Hi, I'm Toye. I am a researcher and engineer working at the intersection of edge AI, computer vision, digital forensics, and cloud systems.

I figure out how systems work — and how they break. My background is in electrical engineering, and I like connecting ideas across hardware, software, and data.

My research focuses on deploying lightweight vision models to resource-constrained environments and building practical AI systems. On the engineering side, I build serverless cloud infrastructure, write forensic tooling, and experiment with agentic systems.

I work mostly in Python, Go, and AWS. When I am not building or researching, I am usually reading about space exploration or planning my next project.

This site is my personal lab — a place to document projects, share what I learn, and test new ideas. Take a look around.
